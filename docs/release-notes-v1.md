# 🎀 Maison Éthique — First Portfolio Release (v1.0)

**November 2026 | A complete, self-contained local portfolio case study; Google Data Studio visualizations will continue in a later iteration.**

## Current dashboard showcase

Four visuals have been built in Google Data Studio, using the project's synthetic source sheets:

1. **Executive KPI cards** — 24 AI systems; 134 open findings; average internal risk 56.42.
2. **Internal AI risk distribution** — 11 Medium (45.8%), 7 High (29.2%), 6 Low (25%).
3. **Average internal risk by department** — Payments 78, Clienteling 72, People 71 lead the simulated portfolio.
4. **Governance control status by framework** — segmented assessments for NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act and GDPR considerations, distinguishing Implemented / Partial / Missing / Not assessed.

**Dataset audit:** 24 fictional AI systems · 264 control assessments · 104 implemented · 134 partial-or-missing · 2,400 fictional fairness decisions.

## Scope and limitations

All company data, case outcomes, system scores and control statuses are **synthetic**. Internal priority tiers are not legal risk classifications. Control-assessment labels do not assert compliance with GDPR or the EU AI Act. NIST AI RMF provides voluntary guidance, and legal obligations are context dependent.

The Data Studio report is authored separately; these charts and the live report have **not yet been published publicly from Google Data Studio**. The completed local ZIP includes the source code, reproducible data, tests, HTML dashboard and the four current report screenshots.
